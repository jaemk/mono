Lay the watch dial down on a hard surface beside the microphone, in a quiet room. A wired or contact microphone works best; turn off headphones' noise cancelling. Readings start after a few seconds of steady beats. Ticks should peak past the amber mark on the input meter; if they don't, move the watch closer or raise the sensitivity. Faint ticks read steadier with a longer averaging window. Sound card clocks can be off by a few s/d: time a known-good watch and enter the difference as mic correction.

To measure without watching it live, press record 30 s: it listens for 30 seconds, then reads the rate over the whole recording and offers it to save. Analyze file does the same for an audio file you already have, or drop the file on the page. Changing a setting reads the same audio again.

To regulate, time the watch in a few positions and save each result under positions; the delta between them shows how evenly it runs. Big readout shows the rate large enough to read while you work on the watch. Keys: space starts or stops the mic, r records, f toggles the big readout.
